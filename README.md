## How to use

1. Copy a video link from TikTok, Instagram or Facebook.
2. Open VIDEOGO on your device.
3. Paste the link and press **Download**.
4. The video is saved to your Downloads folder on Windows, or `Download/VIDEOGO/` on Android.
